# Neiro Canon Album Research Report

Purpose: seed albums for onboarding favorites and cold-start homepage ranking. Prestige is scored as: **1 = undisputed classic**, **2 = critically acclaimed**, **3 = notable**. Scores are intentionally conservative; albums with weak canonical evidence were excluded.

Source shorthand used in tables: **KPM100** = Melon/Hankyoreh/Taillimscore Korean Popular Music Top 100; **KMA** = Korean Music Awards; **MMA/MAMA** = Melon/Mnet Album of the Year history; **IZM/Weiv** = Korean critic/publication coverage; **RSJ** = Rolling Stone Japan 100 Greatest Japanese Rock Albums; **RS500** = Rolling Stone 500 Greatest Albums; **P4K** = Pitchfork decade/canon lists; **Grammy** = Grammy Album of the Year or Hall of Fame/canon relevance.
