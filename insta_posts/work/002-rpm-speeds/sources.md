- ✅ Vitaphone (1926): 16" discs at 33⅓, ~10 min to match a 1,000-ft reel — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitaphone · https://www.mixonline.com/technology/1926-western-electricbell-labs-vitaphone-film-sound-383582

- ⚠️ "78 − 33 = 45" — popular story, no primary evidence; presented as unconfirmed
