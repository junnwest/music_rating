# Sources — 009 Roland TR-808

- ✅ 1980–1983, US$1,195, ~12,000 units, analog synthesis — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_TR-808 · https://vintageking.com/blog/celebrating-808-day/
- ✅ Commercial failure at launch; competitors used samples (e.g. Linn LM-1) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_TR-808 · https://www.perfectcircuit.com/signal/roland-tr-808
- ✅ Cheap on the used market → adopted by young producers — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_TR-808
- ✅ "Planet Rock" (1982) and "Sexual Healing" (1982) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_TR-808 · https://vintageking.com/blog/celebrating-808-day/
- ✅ TR-909 (1983) → house/techno — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_TR-909
- ✅ 808 kick as bass; Kanye West 808s & Heartbreak (2008) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_TR-808 · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/808s_%26_Heartbreak
