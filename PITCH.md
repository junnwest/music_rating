# sillajuku — Project Pitch

## What is it?

sillajuku is a music rating app built for people who take their listening seriously — especially fans of Korean, Japanese, and Asian music who have been underserved by the tools that exist today.

You can rate any album on a half-star scale, write your thoughts on it, and build up a profile that reflects your actual taste over time. Think of it like Letterboxd, but for albums — and with a real focus on the artists and scenes that most Western music apps barely acknowledge.

---

## The problem it solves

If you're deep into K-pop, K-indie, K-hip-hop, or J-pop, the mainstream music apps don't speak your language. Spotify and Apple Music are great for listening, but they're not built for discovery through *community* — you can't see what your friends actually think of an album, you can't compare your taste against theirs, and there's no way to build a meaningful record of your listening history.

Existing rating platforms like RateYourMusic are text-heavy and intimidating, skew heavily Western, and feel like they were designed in 2005.

sillajuku is built from the ground up for the kind of listener who has opinions.

---

## What can you do on it?

**Rate and review albums**
Give any album a score from 0.5 to 5 stars. Leave a comment, set who can see it (public, friends only, or private), and like other people's takes.

**Build your profile**
Your profile shows everything: your ratings grid, your average score, your favorite genres, your "Essentials" — a curated strip of six albums that define your taste. Over time it becomes a real portrait of who you are as a listener.

**Follow friends and see what they're hearing**
The activity feed shows what people you follow are rating in real time. You can see where your taste overlaps with theirs — and where it clashes.

**Taste Collisions and Contradictions**
Two of the more fun features: *Collisions* shows you albums where you and a friend rated things wildly differently (great for arguments). *Contradictions* shows where your score sits against the community average — the albums you loved that nobody else did, and vice versa.

**Community Rankings**
Vote on leaderboards like "Greatest Album of All Time" or "Best K-Hip-Hop Album." Rankings are built from real votes and a seeded baseline, so they're meaningful from day one and get better as more people participate.

**Listen Later**
Save albums you want to get to. A simple list, no friction.

**Yearly Wrapped**
At the end of the year, see a summary of your listening: how many albums you rated, your top genre, your highest and lowest scores, your most active month.

## Who is it for?

- Korean music fans who want a home that actually knows the artists they listen to
- Music obsessives who want to track and reflect on their taste over time
- People who love comparing notes with friends about what they're hearing
- Anyone who's felt like existing rating platforms don't quite fit

---

## Why now?

The global appetite for Korean music — and Asian music more broadly — has never been bigger. But the tools for engaging with it seriously still lag behind. sillajuku is being built at exactly the right moment to become the go-to platform for this community.

---

## Where things stand

The core experience is built and working — you can rate albums, follow friends, vote on rankings, and explore the full social layer today. We're currently in the final stretch before public launch: polishing the UI, adding Korean language support, and wrapping the web app into a native iOS and Android app. Launch is targeted for mid-2026.
